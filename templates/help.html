How to Upload Images

Click on "Select Images" or drag and drop leaf images. Supports JPG, PNG, and WebP formats. You can upload multiple images at once.

camera_alt

Using Camera

Switch to the Camera tab, click "Start Camera", position the leaf clearly in frame, and click "Capture Photo" to take a picture.

analytics

Understanding Results

Analysis results include plant type, health status, disease identification, symptoms, treatment recommendations, medicine suggestions with prices, and cost estimates.

download

Exporting Reports

Click "Download Report" on any analysis to export a PDF with complete details. You can also export multiple reports from the history page.

tips_and_updates

Best Practices

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of affected leaves
  • Capture both top and bottom of leaves if possible
  • Include multiple affected areas for better diagnosis
  • Avoid blurry or dark images
